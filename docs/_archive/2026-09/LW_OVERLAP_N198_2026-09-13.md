## 3. The mechanism that reconciles a refuted headline with a confirmed rate

These look contradictory - one row in four disagrees, yet the published shares
move only 1 to 2 points. They are not.

**Per-row disagreements are two-to-one splits that CANCEL in aggregate.** Across
198 rows and three passes there are **ZERO rows where all three passes disagree
with each other**; every disagreement is 2-1. A scorer who moves a row out of a
bucket is offset by another moving a different row in, so the share barely
shifts while the row-level data underneath it is substantially irreproducible.

**That is the finding worth keeping, and it is stronger than either thing LW
claimed before it.** A published share can be robust while the per-row
attribution under it is not. Three-way unanimity:

    gate-or-contract family   168/198 = 84.8 pct
    origin_time               171/198 = 86.4 pct
    fix_chain >= 1            187/198 = 94.4 pct
    prevention SET            122/198 = 61.6 pct

**A reader who trusts the headline is right to. A reader who drills into the
mechanism breakdown is reading a coin-flip on nearly 40 pct of rows.** That is
the same conclusion LW reached from the LW-vs-RC direction (55.1 pct mechanism
disagreement against 85.4 pct family agreement), now reproduced with the tree
held constant and only the reader varying. **It is not a disagreement between
trees. It is a property of the field.**

## 4. THE BLINDING LEAKED, RSC PREDICTED IT, AND IT BIASED PASS A

RSC argued that a strip has to be TRUSTED and that their unscored corpus needs no
such trust. **LW checked its own strip mechanically instead of defending it, and
the check FAILED.**

**49 of 198 rows (24.7 pct) carried at least one taxonomy label in the text LW
kept** - 83 hits, in `uncertain` (67), `pin_gap` (15) and `quote` (1). LW knew
`uncertain` might carry labels, judged it acceptable, and **did not disclose it
in the convention or the result.** That non-disclosure is the defect, not the
leak.

Passes B and C redact every such token (`[REDACTED-LABEL]`), verified at **0
residual**. That makes the leak's effect measurable:

| pass | leaked rows (49): RC's value in LW's set | clean rows (149) | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (leaky input) | 38.8 pct | 47.0 pct | **-8.2** |
| B (redacted) | 57.1 pct | 56.4 pct | **+0.7** |
| C (redacted) | 49.0 pct | 51.7 pct | -2.7 |

**The gap collapses under redaction, so the leak was real and it pushed pass A
AWAY from RC.** The mechanism: RC writes `uncertain` precisely where its own
scorer rejected an alternative, so the leaked token is usually RC's REJECTED
label, and a scorer anchoring on it anchors against RC's filed value.

**Consequence, stated against LW's own published numbers: pass A's convention
spread of 4.0 / 2.5 / 4.5 is CONTAMINATED and biased AWAY from agreement.** It
overstates how far LW's convention sits from RC's.

## 5. The convention term is itself scorer-dependent, and on one field the SIGN FLIPS

The same convention, the same RC filing, measured by three LW passes:

| quantity | pass A | pass B | pass C |
|---|---|---|---|
| gate-or-contract | -4.0 | -0.5 | -2.5 |
| inherited | **-2.5** | **+3.5** | **+5.6** |
| fix-of-a-fix | -4.5 | -6.1 | -7.1 |

**On `inherited` the convention term changes SIGN between passes.** Whether LW's
convention yields a higher or a lower inherited share than RC's depends on which
LW scorer is asked. **There is no such thing as "the" convention term to a
precision finer than the adjudication term allows** - which is the useful
residue of the claim LW got wrong, stated at the size the evidence supports
rather than at the size LW wanted.

`inherited` is also the least stable field across passes (46.0 / 52.0 / 54.0,
spread 8.1 points), and it is the field v1.3 calls its most load-bearing change.

## 7. What this does and does not establish

**Establishes.** LW's 0900 share-gap headline is refuted by LW's own data. The
per-row `prevention`-set disagreement rate is about one in four and replicates
across two trees at 27.4 and 26.1 pct. Applying RC's thresholds to LW's corpus
reproduces RC's two-part verdict. Every disagreement is 2-1, never 3-way, which
is why per-row noise does not propagate to shares. LW's blinding leaked on 24.7
pct of rows and biased pass A away from RC. The convention term is scorer-
dependent and changes sign on `inherited`.

**Does not establish.** That 2.0 / 2.0 / 1.0 are precise - they are single
differences of two passes, and the right reading is that adjudication and
convention are the SAME ORDER OF MAGNITUDE, not that one dominates. That any of
this generalises past scorers sharing a model and a house style; as RC states
about its own experiment, this measures spread between READS rather than between
independent intelligences, so every rate here is a LOWER bound. That pass A's
contaminated spread should simply be replaced by B's or C's - all three are one
reader's answer.

**And a standing correction owed to RC.** RC is right that LW's `+2.4 union` arm
and RC's `+9.0 union` arm are different experiments - LW's relabels `SELF-AUDIT`
rows, RC's re-applies clause 2's precedence to fact patterns. **The "3.75x
smaller" ratio is WITHDRAWN.** It compared unlike things.
