## Defect 1 - off-colour: what it is NOT, and what it is

- NOT a chromatic veil. Per-channel fit I = (1 - a_c) J + a_c W_c inside the
  logo (sRGB): C a = 0.1465 / 0.1415 / 0.1490, W = 249 / 253 / 247; C1920
  0.1444 / 0.1411 / 0.1441. With W fixed at 250 the per-channel alpha
  differs by under 1 percent (A 0.1440 / 0.1423 / 0.1427, B 0.1480 / 0.1456
  / 0.1479, C 0.1461 / 0.1435 / 0.1460): G ~0.002 low on all three - a
  0.4-level effect. Applying the per-channel gains (A+B, held-out C)
  measured WORSE (deltaE inside 1.86 -> 1.89 with the field). Not shipped.
- NOT gamma. A linear-light compositing model fits worse than sRGB on every
  frame (C rms 5.05 / 4.86 / 4.76 vs 3.51 / 3.36 / 3.44 levels; A, B alike)
  and leaves a tone-dependent residual of -4..+4.5 levels across luma
  quintiles, where the sRGB model's is flat (C within +-0.5). The veil is
  composited in sRGB; the linear inversion is right.
- NOT JPEG 4:2:0 at the fill: the per-channel fits above agree; chroma
  subsampling acts only at the edge, which the LaMa ring covers.
- IT IS the alpha. The pass-3 pre-pass leaves the interior under-removed on
  average (C +1.5 / +1.2 / +1.3 levels, B +1.8 / +1.6 / +1.8) and with a
  strong top-to-foot trend (plane fit of the residual vs height: -11..-26
  levels per frame height on A, B, C and C1920 alike). An alpha error e
  leaves e (W - J_c) per channel - on saturated art a whitish (under) or
  over-saturated (over) TINT, which is what reads as "off colour"; the trend
  is the "shading". Defects 1 and 2 are one cause.

## Defect 2 - shading / inconsistency: a static alpha field

True fill alpha per matte-grid cell (luma, a = sum d (I - J) / sum d^2,
d = W - J > 40), smoothed 12 ref px:

- it FALLS from the logo's top to its foot on every frame: plane fit on the
  grid, alpha = c + s y + t x: A 0.165 - 0.040 y, B 0.161 - 0.033 y, C
  0.163 - 0.037 y (y = 0..1 over the grid; x terms <= 0.007). p5 / p95 inside
  the logo 0.124 / 0.152 (A), 0.126 / 0.163 (B), 0.124 / 0.152 (C);
- it is STATIC: field correlation A-C 0.976, B-C 0.841, A-B 0.833; C vs
  C1920 0.995; C's rms about its mean 0.0088, about the A+B field 0.0033;
- the per-frame scalar fit (read at the edge, `fit_pair_alpha`) sits UNDER
  the true alpha of the edge strip by kappa = 1.040 (A), 1.111 (B), 1.084
  (C) - the interior under-removal above.
- tone: the pre-pass residual is flat across luma quintiles on C (+1.2..+1.9)
  - no tone curve needed. Halo: the residual in the 3-24 ref px band is
  -0.4..-0.8 levels on every frame (the pass-2 halo inversion holds).

Fix (`tools/lw_clean_overlay.py` PASS-4 block, `lw_clean_iopaint --pass4`):
`pair_true_alpha_sums` (builder only) + `build_pair_alpha_field`: the pooled
true alpha on the grid, smoothed 12 cells, carried off the logo by nearest
value, normalized to median 1 over the edge strip the fit reads (2-6 ref px
inside), times the mean kappa of the training frames; bounded 0.6..1.6.
`render_pair_ratio` / `pair_alpha_field_crop` put it on the frame under the
lane's registration; `remove_pair_edge(alpha_field=)` and
`pair_ring_envelope(alpha_field=)` multiply the fitted alpha per pixel (fill
and halo). The edge fit and offsets keep the scalar alpha (the pass-3
geometry, as validated). `tools/lw_veil_alpha_field.py` builds the
production asset `ops/runtime/clean/da_veil_pair_alpha_field.npz`
(gitignored, numbers only) from A + B ONLY: kappa 1.0403 / 1.1112, mean
1.0758; field p5 / p95 on the logo 0.900 / 1.102. Default None = pass 3 byte
for byte (pinned).

Pre-pass only, held-out (C, C1920 from A+B; A from B+C; B from A+C), true
low-frequency error = RMS of the 12 ref px Gaussian-smoothed residual inside
the logo (levels), and deltaE76 inside:

| frame | scalar LF / deltaE / top-foot slope | field LF / deltaE / slope |
|---|---|---|
| C 2560 | 1.83 / 1.84 / -11..-15 | 0.54 / 1.67 / -0.7..+0.2 |
| C 1920 | 1.54 / 1.97 / -12..-16 | 0.56 / 1.86 / -1.7..+0.1 |
| B 2560 | 2.32 / 2.31 / -17..-21 | 1.02 / 2.12 / -0.1..+1.0 |
| A 2560 | 2.21 / 3.14 / -24..-27 | 1.96 / 3.14 / -1.9..+0.2 |

(C's deltaE floor - the same measure outside the veil - is 1.65: the field
takes the interior to within 0.02 of it.) A's mean is over-removed by ~1.8
levels with the B+C kappa (A's own kappa is the lowest, 1.04): the slope is
gone, the level is not better. Recorded, not hidden.

## Independent metric: `tools/lw_veil_shading.py`

`boundary_step` reads the OUTPUT frame and the coverage only (never the fit
or the field - pinned by a test that makes them raise): windows every 8 ref
px along the contour; in each, CIELAB pixels of an inside band (3-12 ref px,
past the LaMa ring) and an outside band (-12..-4 ref px) within 16 ref px; a
plane (art gradient) plus an inside STEP per channel; smooth-art weight
1 / (residual variance + 4); per third of the logo height the weighted median
step; `step_de_rms` = RMS over the thirds of the step's length (deltaE76).
A first version with wide bands (6-18 in / 24-40 out) read the art itself
(clean C 10.5) and was replaced.

Validation (`diag/val_shading_{A,B,C}.json`): 15 candidates per pair - the
pre-pass with the alpha scaled 0.85-1.15, a top-to-foot gradient of +-15 /
30 percent, the field at 0.95 / 1.00 / 1.05 - against the true low-frequency
deltaE: Spearman 0.91 (A), 0.96 (B), 0.86 (C). Clean frames read 0.59 / 0.48
/ 0.53; the veiled inputs 14.3 / 11.0 / 9.1. Floor on the 30 veil-free frames
of pass 2: median 0.74, p90 2.53, p95 3.17, max 4.24 ->
`SHADING_GATE` 3.2 (p95, the same convention as the other two gates).
Reported per slug, not used to select.

## Defect 3 - the credit line

Measured on the pairs (`diag/credit*.json`): the credit line is the logo's
material - a semi-transparent white fill (alpha 0.140 C, 0.143 B, 0.152 A,
read against LaMa's background) with a dark rim (outline on C, drop shadow on
B; black alpha up to ~0.3 next to the glyph) - in a FIXED row 0.06-0.13 logo
heights below the logo's foot (C 0.072-0.126, A 0.063-0.126, B 0.083-0.127)
and CENTRED on the logo's column (C spans -0.43..+0.45 logo heights, A
-0.66..+0.62, B -0.38..+0.49). Only the length varies.

How pass 3 handled it (unchanged since the wire slice): the collection
matte's seed below the logo plus `complete_overlay_mask`'s bright completion,
then LaMa. At 2x on six slugs: the first and last glyphs fell outside it on
most ("(c) S" and "M" left on 261f and 262f, "(c) M" on bamboo-gal), and on bright
art the completion missed whole words (seraphine-by-pebano1: "PEBANO1" half
removed, "DEVIANTART.COM" untouched; 224f "LUCAS66..." left as a ghost).

Fix (`tools/lw_veil_credit.py`, wired in `clean_slug(pass4=True)`): the row
from the registered logo; the half-width = the lane's own detection's
farthest column from the logo's centre inside that row, on EITHER side, plus
0.12 logo heights, applied to BOTH sides (bounded 0.40-1.10 logo heights);
the whole text box replaces the old seed around the row and goes to LaMa; the
logo's edge ring is re-asserted after (the foot sits just above).

Tried and rejected (truth C / B / A, `diag/val_credit2.json`,
`val_credit3.json`, crops `diag/credit2_*`, `credit3_*`):

- per-glyph analytic de-blend - LaMa's fill as the background, glyph cores
  inverted J = (I - a W) / (1 - a) with the frame's own a, art between
  glyphs kept, edges and rim clamped to the veil band: best text PSNR (C
  28.6 vs input 26.9 vs whole-box LaMa 23.4) but the dark OUTLINE stays
  visible on every pair (the rim's black alpha varies along each glyph; the
  physical clamp holds it) - fails the zero-residue bar;
- a glyph-accurate mask (fill + rim read from LaMa's first fill, dilated 2-5
  px), LaMa again or the first fill composited: art kept (art PSNR 40-46 vs
  31-37) but fragments stay wherever the first fill's background guess is
  wrong (C's "(c) NA" over hair, B's "S", "TAR" over the lips).

Whole-box LaMa is the only variant with no visible text on all three pairs.
Its cost is LaMa's: the art under a ~37 px row is invented (C: a small smear
where hair crosses the row; B: the lip line under the text softened). That is
the trade the manual IOPaint lane makes too; the review crops show it per
slug.

## Hermetic tests

`tests/test_lw_veil_pass4.py` (12): a veil with an independent linear
top-to-foot alpha fall and a fit bias, composited on synthetic art; the field
learned from two frames (shift / art differ) recovers the fall and the bias
and removes the top-to-foot shading on a held-out frame (< 35 percent of the
scalar inversion's); `alpha_field=None` is the pass-3 inversion and envelope
byte for byte; the envelope follows the field; `render_pair_ratio` is
unitless and 1 off the window; the field file round-trips and a bad one is
refused; `overlay_prepass(alpha_field=)` default is pass 3 and records the
field; the shading metric ranks injected alpha errors and reads clean art
under the gate, and never calls the fit, the field or the inversion; a
synthetic credit line (glyph blocks, white fill 0.145, dark rim) - the box
holds every glyph and its rim when the detection saw only one half (either
half); `clean_slug(pass4=True)` end to end with a stub inpainter masks the
whole credit line and keeps the logo ring.

Mutation-proven: the field not multiplied in the inversion, kappa forced to
1, the mirrored reach replaced by one side's, and the prepass not passing the
field each turn a test red. Not bound: the re-assertion of the logo ring
after the credit zone is cleared (the zone starts below the ring's reach on
every frame measured - defensive only).
