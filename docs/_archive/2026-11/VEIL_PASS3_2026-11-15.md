## Verdict

- Three root causes, all measured against ground truth: (1) the pair
  matte's contour is off by a STATIC, location-dependent amount (-0.6..+0.8
  ref px, largest at the tip's bay end), which the per-frame offset only
  half follows -> the tip dot and much of the faint per-side line; (2) the
  registration SCALE was never refined (search steps 0.04; the upscaled
  frames sit at ~1.005) -> blood-moon's misfit and per-side offsets; (3) LaMa
  in a 5 px ring along a shallow art edge smears colour -> the blotches.
- Fix: a static edge-correction field learned from the purchased pairs
  (A + B; C held out), a coarse-to-fine scale refinement, and the LaMa ring
  fill clamped to the veil model's band (never narrower than pre-pass +- 16
  levels). Opt-in `--pass3`; defaults byte-identical.
- Truth: edge PSNR +1.0..+3.6 dB on every frame incl. held-out C (2560
  29.19 -> 31.94, 1920 28.98 -> 31.60), C ghost 0.64 -> 0.38; costs logo

## Root causes (measured)

Instrument: the purchased pairs A/B/C pushed through the real first-pass
chain to 2560 (pass 2's `truth/`), plus the held-out C at 1920. Scratch
outputs: `images/_veil_pairs/2026-11-15/pass3/diag/` (gitignored).

1. Tip dot (dark dot at the left-arm tip, i.e. the rounded END of the bay
   the logo wraps around). With the clean frame known, the model error
   `I - [J (1 - aP - G) + a W P]` in the tip window peaks at -14..-16
   levels (C) / -11..-13 (B) on pixels at sd +0.4..+1.7 px, i.e. INSIDE
   the logo just past the edge, where P = 0.3-0.9: the model's fill reaches
   ~0.7 px too far into the bay there (16 / (a (W - J)) = 0.7). Over-removal
   -> 2-3 dark pixels at the LaMa ring's inner boundary (+1.33 px), partly
   outside the mask; LaMa grows them into the dot. Not a halo-superposition
   effect (the halo error there is <= 2 levels).
2. Why the per-frame offset did not catch it: the TRUE local edge offset
   along the whole contour (read against the clean frame, per contour pixel)
   is ~85 percent STATIC. Leave-one-out between pairs (field from two pairs
   predicting the third, pooled 5 ref px): correlation 0.86 / 0.87 / 0.90,
   rms 0.50-0.60 ref px, prediction error 0.26-0.29 ref px; the pass-2
   per-frame field (aggregated 10 ref px along the contour, read from the
   art) tracks the truth only at corr 0.66-0.79 (error 0.33-0.43). The
   matte's contour is simply off by a location-dependent -0.6..+0.8 ref px
   (p10/p90), largest at tight features. This is also the faint top-edge
   line: per-side wander that is the same on every frame.
3. Scale. The pair search steps scale by 0.04 and nothing refined it:
   blood-moon was registered at 1.08 for a gradient score that peaks at
   1.065 (390 -> 495) - the contour off by up to +-2 px across the logo, and
   the edge fit absorbed it with dy and d0 both at their bounds (+1.5 /
   -1.5) and sigma 1.19: the "worse, new dark stroke" slug. All three truth
   pairs at 2560 peak at ~1.005 (the upscale chain), which is most of pass
   2's "top edges ~1 px out, bottom ~0-0.5 px in" and of its d0 0.6-0.7 (d0
   drops to 0.14-0.26 once the scale is refined).
4. Blotches (right arm's bottom edge). The ring is a 5 px strip; where it
   lies along a strong art edge crossing at a shallow angle (32-cleanup's
   black band over red-brown, the truth C purple tip) LaMa cannot place that
   edge and smears colour across it - tens of levels away from the
   algebraic pre-pass, which there is right to within a few levels.

## The fix (`tools/lw_clean_overlay.py` PASS-3 block, `lw_clean_iopaint --pass3`)

- `pair_true_edge_offsets` (builder only, needs the clean frame),
  `pair_grid_coords` / `render_pair_grid`, `build_pair_edge_corr`,
  `save_ / load_pair_edge_corr`: a static correction field on the matte grid
  (ref px, + outward), learned from truth pairs by
  `tools/lw_veil_edge_corr.py`; `pair_signed_distance(edge_corr=...)` adds
  it to the signed distance; the per-frame fit and offsets then run on top.
  The production asset `ops/runtime/clean/da_veil_pair_edge_corr.npz`
  (gitignored, numbers only) is built from A + B ONLY, so C stays held out.
- `refine_pair_scale`: coarse-to-fine scale (+-0.03, steps 0.01 then
  0.0025, parabola), shift re-refined at each; used by pass 3 and by the
  builder, so the static field is learned in the same frame.
- `pair_ring_envelope` + `bound_ring_fill` (`IO.apply_ring_bound`): the
  LaMa ring fill is clamped, inside the ring and the mask only, to the
  de-veiled values the model allows with the edge moved +-0.75 px and the
  halo +-50 percent, and never narrower than the pre-pass +- 16 levels
  (PAIR_RING_TOL). A purely physical band (tol 0) was tried first and
  rejected: on 261f it clamped the fill back onto the residual line LaMa had
  removed (top corner line visible, ghost 0.57 -> 0.66; tol 8 0.54, tol 16
  0.52, tol 24 0.52, unbounded 0.52; on 32-cleanup tol 0/8/16/24/unbounded
  1.44/1.44/1.47/1.72/2.16, pass 2 1.79). 16 levels is the largest
  pre-pass residue measured on the truth pairs (the bay end before the fix).
- Defaults are the pass-2 path byte for byte (pinned).
- Found on the first live run: `clean_slug` reused the name `region` for the
  clamp's boolean map, which then went into the manifest params and broke
  json.dumps; renamed and pinned by an end-to-end test with a stub
  inpainter.
