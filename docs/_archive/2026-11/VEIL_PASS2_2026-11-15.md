# DA logo veil - pass 2: border ghost root cause, edge-ghost metric, edge-profile fix (2026-11-15)

Follows `VEIL_RESID_GATE_2026-11-15.md` (batch 3, LEDGER 373). Operator order
2026-11-15: "review the need auths - they need one more pass to remove the
ghosting and artifacts; it is dominantly the border - keep the need auths as
great progress is made, but address the ghosting and artifacts".

## Verdict

- Root cause (measured, not guessed): the pair matte's EDGE does not match the
  upscaled frame's veil edge, and the veil's dark halo was never inverted.
  On the purchased pairs run through the real first-pass chain the veil edge
  is ~1 px wide where the matte's ramp is ~3 px, it sits 0.5-1 px OUTSIDE the
  matte's 0.5 contour, ~0.3-0.4 px off vertically, its position wobbles by up
  to +-1 px per edge segment against the consensus shape, and the dark halo
  outside it reaches ~20 px. The lane left a bright 2 px line on the edge
  (+0.04-0.05 alpha, under the LaMa ring), a faint bright line just inside the
  ring (+0.01-0.02 at +1.5 px) and a dark band outside the ring (black alpha
  0.03-0.06 at 4-8 px). LaMa then filled the 1/3 ring from that dark and
  bright context.
- Fix (`lw_clean_overlay` PASS-2 EDGE PROFILE): sub-pixel signed distance to
  the matte contour; per frame fit of shift, outward offset, edge blur and
  halo amplitude / reach (alpha stays the validated `fit_pair_alpha`); a
  per-pixel offset along the contour; inversion of fill AND halo; LaMa ring
  cut from the fitted edge. Opt-in (`overlay_prepass(edge_fit=True)`,
  `lw_clean_iopaint.py --overlay --edge-fit`).
- Metric (`tools/lw_veil_ghost.py`): ranks injected edge errors as the ground
  truth does; gate 2.0 levels (veil-free floor p95).
- Held-out C does not regress: full lane at preview res logo 34.34 -> 34.78,
  edge 28.86 -> 29.64, near 34.54 -> 35.15, far 39.27 -> 39.28 dB.
- The current NEEDAUTH submissions were not touched. Pass 2 is an ADDITIONAL
  candidate per slug (storage section).

## Step 1 - root cause, measured on the truth pairs

Fill alpha by signed distance to the matte contour (2560 px, + inside;
`truth/edge_profile_up.json`, `edge_profile_fine.json`):

- true veil: ~0 at -1.5 px, 0.09-0.10 at -0.5, 0.13-0.14 at +0.5, full by
  +1.5: a ~1 px edge whose 50 percent point is outside the matte contour;
- matte as registered: 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.13 at -1.5 / -0.5 / +0.5 /
  +1.5: a ~3 px ramp;
- lane pre-pass residual: +0.040..+0.049 at -0.5 and +0.5, +0.013..+0.024 at
  +1.5 (inside the ring's inner edge at +1.33 px), -0.02..-0.08 outside.
- by edge orientation (`truth/` orient run): top edges sit ~1 px out, bottom
  edges ~0-0.5 px in on all three pairs - a residual vertical offset the
  gradient-correlation refine did not remove; left/right edges differ by
  ~0.3-0.7 px; after a global fit, per-orientation residuals of +-4..12
  levels remain at single edges = per-segment contour wobble.
- halo, black-layer alpha outside: 0.06-0.09 at 3-4 px, 0.05 at 6, 0.04 at
  10, 0.02-0.03 at 16-20 (A/B/C at 2560); in ref px it matches C at 1920
  (0.019 vs 0.018 at 10 ref px): a property of the veil, scaled with the
  frame, not an upscaler artifact. The lane never inverted it (by design
  since a2eb6c3) and the 1/3 ref px ring covers only its first 4 px.

So: edge profile mismatch (width and position) + per-segment contour error +
uninverted halo. Sub-pixel registration explains a part (the vertical
offset), not all; the ring is not "too narrow" - widening it (2/4) lowers the
line ghost but costs 1-2 dB against the truth (LaMa invents).

## Step 2 - edge-ghost metric (`tools/lw_veil_ghost.py`)

Per edge-normal octant x side bin (outer -5..-2.5, out -2.5..0, in 0..1.5,
inner 1.5..3.5 ref px), the smooth-art-weighted MEDIAN of the luma high-pass;
`line_rms` = RMS over the cells (levels). Independent of the fix: it reads
the OUTPUT frame's high-pass along the contour; the fix fits a planar-art
window model to the INPUT. It never calls a fit (pinned by
`test_never_calls_the_fit`). The prototype and the module agree to 3
decimals on the truth frames.

Validation on A/B/C at 2560 (`truth/metric_inject.json`): candidates de-veiled
with injected errors from the fitted optimum - shift 0-3 px, offset +-2 px,
halo 0-100 percent left, blur 0-3 px; truth = RMS(candidate - clean) within
12 ref px of the edge. Spearman(line_rms, truth) over all 23 candidates per
pair: A 0.92, B 0.98, C 0.95; within a family 1.00 except C offset 0.93 and
A halo -0.60 (A is dark art: its true error moves 10.49 -> 10.62 over the
whole halo family). Clean truth frames read 0.33 / 0.17 / 0.11; the veiled
inputs 5.3 / 4.5 / 4.2; the lane pre-pass 2.5 / 2.7 / 3.2.

Noise floor (`pass2/ghost_floor.json`): 30 veil-free frames (the 16 batch-1
veil-free + 14 cleandone, the resid-gate set) at the default registration:
median 0.19, p90 1.22, p95 2.00, max 6.36 (spirit-blossom-ahri: heavy ink
lines along the logo's edge directions). Gate GHOST_LINE_GATE = 2.0 (p95, the
same convention as the 0.02 alpha gate). The halo statistic is reported, not
gated: on the floor frames |halo| reaches p95 4.3 levels on art alone. Also
measured and rejected: a per-cell (16 ref px) local statistic - its floor is
p95 6.6-8.3 levels, so local residue cannot be separated from art without a
reference; localized residue is left to the operator's eye.

## Step 3 - the fix

`tools/lw_clean_overlay.py` PASS-2 EDGE PROFILE:

- `pair_signed_distance`: contour = 0.5 level of the registered coverage
  rendered 4x finer; sub-pixel distance near the edge, frame-res beyond.
- `fit_pair_edge`: theta = (dy, dx, d0, sigma, halo_k, halo_t), coverage
  P = area profile of a hard edge at sd - n.(dy,dx) + d0, blurred sigma; halo
  G = a k PAIR_HALO(d / t). Objective: in windows (8 ref px half-width)
  centred within 10 ref px of the edge, the de-veiled luma must be a plane;
  robust loss mean log(sse + median start sse) over ALL windows. (A fixed
  "smoother half" chosen at the start point - the alpha fit's trick - kept
  the windows the start model already explains and found dy -0.03 for a true
  -0.4 on the fixture; replaced.) Coordinate descent, golden section, 3
  rounds.
- `pair_edge_offsets`: per pixel, SSE curves over offsets -1.25..+1.25 px
  aggregated along the contour (10 ref px) and minimized - absorbs the
  per-segment wobble.
- `remove_pair_edge`: J = (I - a W P) / (1 - a P - G); outside the support
  byte-identical; returns the support for G2.
- `lw_clean_iopaint.overlay_prepass(edge_fit=True)` / `clean_slug(...,
  edge_fit=True)` / `--edge-fit`: ring cut from the fitted edge (still 1 in /
  3 out ref px), the support written as `<slug>_edge_support.png` and the
  fitted coverage as `<slug>_edge_cov.npy` for the gates. Default False: the
  current lane is byte-identical (pinned).

Truth, pre-pass only (`truth/prod_truth.json`; edge = +-3 ref px, halo = 3-24
ref px outside, dB vs the upscaled clean; ghost line levels):

- A: edge 25.55 -> 26.29, halo 26.89 -> 26.94, ghost 2.50 -> 1.12

- C at 1920: edge 29.68 -> 34.08, halo 36.18 -> 38.55, ghost 3.69 -> 1.15

`images/_veil_pairs/2026-11-15/pass2/<slug>.png`.

ABOVE the pass-2 working, and `submit` took the highest working - it would
have re-submitted the very bytes the operator just rejected. `cmd_submit`
now skips workings whose sha256 a REJECT transition recorded (same rule as
`lw_clean_pass.select_working_image`, incident 2026-10-04) and refuses when
every working was rejected. Pinned by
`test_submit_after_reject_skips_the_rejected_working` and
`test_submit_refuses_when_every_working_was_rejected`.
