## Defect 1 - credit line bottom edge smudge

Measured (`diag/dbgcredit_*`, `diag/glyph_runs.json`, `diag/credit_det_*`):

- the credit font is NOT one size. On A the glyphs are ~25 px wide and span
  28-57 px under the logo's foot (logo height 460); on B / C ~14 px wide,
  36-56 px. Most slugs carry the BIG font: on meramora the glyphs reach 63 px
  plus a ~3 px drop shadow (0.143 logo heights); pass 4's box (0.055-0.135
  logo heights, +-2 px) ended at 64 px, so its bottom edge cut the glyph
  bottoms and the shadow. LaMa then extends the cut glyph bottoms into the
  box: the dark smear along the box's bottom edge (meramora, soraka,
  seraphine-by-pebano1).
- the half-width: pass 4's "detection reach" was the collection seed's fixed
  extent (0.70 logo heights on most slugs), not the text's. The big-font
  lines run to ~0.72 logo heights from the centre: soraka's "(c)" fell
  half outside the box and LaMa grew it into a grey disc.
- a per-frame text detector (row energy + column excess) was tried: it
  located B / C rows to 1 px but failed on ~15 of 55 inputs (art edges in the
  row band) - not shipped.

Fix (`tools/lw_veil_pass5.py`, `lw_veil_credit.locate_credit(rows=, half=,
seed_rows=)`): box rows 0.050-0.160 logo heights, half-width floor 0.78 logo
heights (bounded 1.10); the detection reach is read in the text's core rows
only (`seed_rows` = pass 4's rows) - reading it in the taller box first widened
C's box to 1.05 logo heights (art marked in the extra rows), caught and pinned by
a test. The credit fill then gets the membrane seam fix (below), as its own
region. Cost, measured on the pairs: a larger LaMa area. C's credit band
(clean-ref PSNR, rows r0-4 .. r1+0.03 lh) 29.26 -> 26.84 dB, A 24.79 -> 22.48,
B 30.15 -> 26.16 - LaMa's invented art in the wider box (C: the runic art left of
the text is smeared further); no text or rim on any (crops
`diag/truth_lane5_{A,B,C}_credit_clean_in_p4_p5.png`, rows clean | input | p4 |
p5). Pass 4 removed C's and B's small-font lines fully; the gain is on the
big-font lines, which the pairs' small-font C does not carry.

## Defect 2 - ghost lines (122, inkshadow) and the ring seams

Measured on 122 (cross-sections across the left edge, `diag/xsec*`, profile
vs signed distance per edge direction): the visible lines sit at the LaMa
ring's SEAMS (outer seam at -4.75 px, inner at +3.5 px) and as a 1-level
bright / dark pair inside (+2 / +3..+5 px); the ring fill also carries a
colour cast (R-G 17.6-18.0 in the ring vs 16.0-16.8 beside it). On the truth
pairs the pass-4 ring has a GREEN deficit relative to R and B (C: G -1.0..-2.0
levels in the ring band -3..0 ref px; B -0.9..-1.2; A -0.3) that the pre-pass
does not have (pre-pass ring bias within +-0.7 and not chromatic) - LaMa's
fill colour drift, read as a magenta outline on plain background.

Fixes (`tools/lw_veil_pass5.py`, run after LaMa and the pass-3 ring clamp,
`clean_slug(pass5=True)` / `lw_clean_iopaint --pass5`):

1. `membrane_fill`: out = fill + c inside each LaMa region, c harmonic, its
   boundary value the step between each outside pixel and the fill next to it,
   clipped to +-6 levels, art-edge down-weighted (1 / (|grad|^2 + 4)),
   smoothed along the boundary (9 px). Adds a smooth field only. Applied to the
   ring part of the mask (within 12 px of the contour) and to the credit box's
   part, separately.
2. `contour_profile_fix`: per 32 px cell, a quadratic art surface plus a tent
   profile p(d) of the sub-pixel signed distance (knots -5..+5 px), smooth-art
   weighted, Tukey IRLS; a cell whose profile departs from its 3x3 neighbours'
   median by more than 1 level takes the median (coherence along the
   contour); capped at 3 levels; subtracted on the declared support (edge
   support OR LaMa mask) inside the ROI.

(`diag/truth_lane5_{A,B,C}.json`; dB vs the colour-matched clean frame):

## Defect 3 - hue at the veil edge on background

Same cause as the ring cast above (LaMa's green deficit in the ring, plus the
seam steps); fixed by the membrane + profile. The interior's low-frequency
colour is the alpha field's (pass 4); a further interior harmonization was
measured worse (above).

Independent metric `tools/lw_veil_hue.py` `ring_hue_step`: reads the OUTPUT
frame and the coverage only (never the pass-5 fixes - pinned by a test); per
window along the contour, CIELAB of the flank bands (-10..-5 and +4..+10 ref
px) fitted with a plane, the ring band's (-4..+3) weighted-median departure;
per octant of the normal a weighted median; `ring_de` = RMS over octants of
the (a*, b*) departure. Validation (`diag/val_hue.json`): 18 candidates per pair
(clean and pass-4 frames with ring tints 0-3 levels in G, R+B, R, B) vs the
true ring chroma error: Spearman 0.986 (A), 0.994 (B), 0.901 (C; C's clean art
itself reads 1.49). Floor on the 30 veil-free frames of pass 2 (`diag/
hue_floor.json`): median 0.33, p90 1.92, p95 2.35, max 3.21 -> `HUE_GATE` 2.4.
Reported per slug, not used to select.

## Hermetic tests

`tests/test_lw_veil_pass5.py` (12): the profile fix removes a coherent outline
(bright inside / dark outside / green deficit) on plain art to under 0.45 of
its RMS, leaves plain art alone (< 0.3 levels), stays bounded on textured art,
never touches pixels outside `allowed`, is capped; the membrane removes a fill
offset (< 0.35 of it) with a smooth correction and does nothing across a
perfect fill with an art edge; the pass-4 box misses a big-font credit line
and its shadow and the pass-5 box holds it; the reach is read in the text rows
only (art marked in the taller box's extra rows does not widen it); the
default `locate_credit` is the pass-4 box; the hue metric ranks ring tints and
never calls the fixes; `clean_slug(pass5=True)` end to end with a drifting stub
inpainter records `pass5` and removes the ring's green deficit, and outside
(edge support OR LaMa mask) equals pass 4 byte for byte.

Mutation-proven: membrane boundary value zeroed, reach read in the box rows,
profile cap removed, pass-4 credit rows, `allowed` ignored - each turns a test
red (restored byte-exact, sha256 checked).
